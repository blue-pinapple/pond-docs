# Ethics Guidlines

If a player died to a bug/glitch/lag or something non-vanilla, and they wouldn't have died otherwise, then their inventory can be restored



If a mob despawned to a non-vanilla thing players can have it restored. e.g. papers entity culling got rid of Axelotal. (Use core-protect to see if it died with \`user:#kill\`)



A hack is considered anything that would give / take a significant advantage over a vanilla player.



Land takeovers are discouraged, but after a minimum of 2 months and a decent attempt at contacting the user, you can consider it.

> I like that there can be old abandoned buildings and bases that dont get touched.
>
> \- goose



Things that are annoying, but still part of the game, should still be preserved.

Vanilla bugs are acceptable unless they are op / dupe rare items, e.g. dupping ender dragon egg is not allowed
