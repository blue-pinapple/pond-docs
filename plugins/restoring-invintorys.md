# Restoring Invintorys

Players that died due to lag can have there invintorys restored

If the glitch was coming out of a world, you do whatever world they went into e.g. If they just went into the end from the overworld, the chest labeled end will show the invintory they had in the overworld.

Then, if they don't have anything on them atm, you can override their inv

or you can just **take them out** and put them in a chest
